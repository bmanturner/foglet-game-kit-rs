# Drop Dead Nebula — Campaign, Seasons, and Dead Gate Deep Dive

Status: Draft v0.1  
Primary milestone relevance: flavor M0, structure M11, polish M12  
Purpose: Define the long arc that turns systems into a season-scale story.

---

## 1. Design Intent

The campaign layer gives long-term meaning to repeated sessions.

It should:

- provide a season arc;
- use existing systems rather than replace them;
- make Faction choices matter;
- unlock dangerous routes and rare content;
- produce a memorable finale;
- support low-pop completion.

It should not:

- be required for MVP fun;
- hard reset without warning;
- invalidate solo careers;
- introduce unrelated endgame mechanics.

---

## 2. Season Phases

### Phase 1: Frontier Scramble

Focus:

- safe trade;
- map discovery;
- first jobs;
- beginner Markets.

Systems:

- MVP + living corridor.

### Phase 2: Route Wars

Focus:

- hazards;
- Factions;
- piracy;
- route control;
- smuggling.

Systems:

- M3, M6, M7, M9.

### Phase 3: Relic Rush

Focus:

- salvage;
- anomalies;
- Saints/Concord conflict;
- rare Commodities.

Systems:

- M4, M7, M8.

### Phase 4: Succession Crisis

Focus:

- Faction fronts;
- shared goals;
- outposts/corps;
- regional stakes.

Systems:

- M7–M10.

### Phase 5: The Dead Gate Opens

Focus:

- final route unlock;
- campaign jobs;
- relic/faction decisions;
- season outcome.

Systems:

- M11.

---

## 3. Dead Gate Design

The Dead Gate begins as a flavor Place and matures into campaign endpoint.

It should require:

- relay repair;
- route stabilization;
- relic/black-box recovery;
- faction alignment or compromise;
- strategic goods;
- player/NPC contributions.

Possible outcomes:

- Gate opens safely;
- Gate opens under Cartel control;
- Gate is sealed by Port Authority;
- Saints awaken something strange;
- Concord automates the route;
- Dust Clans turn it into a pirate corridor.

---

## 4. Campaign Jobs

Campaign jobs are just existing job types with higher stakes:

- deliver strategic goods;
- recover relics;
- clear route hazards;
- defend outposts;
- scan anomalies;
- contribute to shared goals;
- challenge rivals for influence.

---

## 5. Season Outcomes

A season ending should produce:

- final Event Log chronicle;
- leaderboard summary;
- Faction outcome;
- player titles/legacy notes;
- optional world reset/partial reset.

---

## 6. Low-Pop Completion

If few players are active:

- NPCs contribute to campaign goals;
- requirements scale down;
- solo objectives substitute for group totals;
- season can extend automatically;
- final outcome may be narrower but still achievable.

---
