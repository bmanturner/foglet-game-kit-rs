# Drop Dead Nebula — Factions and Shared Goals Deep Dive

Status: Draft v0.1  
Primary milestone relevance: flavor M0, playable M7, campaign maturity M11  
Purpose: Define Factions, standing, influence, shared goals, and world consequences.

---

## 1. Design Intent

Factions let players do more than earn credits. They let players decide who the corridor becomes.

Factions should:

- create ideological choices;
- alter Markets, Routes, jobs, and services;
- support solo contribution;
- create shared goals for low and high population;
- produce visible world consequences;
- provide NPC faces and notices.

Factions should not:

- require guild-level population;
- become abstract meters only;
- lock players permanently too early;
- hide consequences.

## 3. Initial Factions

### Mourning Union

Theme: repair, labor, refugees, humanitarian logistics.  
Benefits: repair discounts, relief jobs, station trust.  
Costs: dislikes piracy/exploitation.

### Helix Cartel

Theme: trade, leverage, smuggling, monopoly.  
Benefits: market intel, black-market access, bribes.  
Costs: law attention, Union distrust.

### Port Authority Black Office

Theme: order, customs, lawful control, secret enforcement.  
Benefits: patrol protection, legal bounties, inspection leniency.  
Costs: contraband restrictions.

### Saints of Vacuum

Theme: relics, anomalies, ritualized exploration.  
Benefits: anomaly access, relic interpretation, weird modules.  
Costs: mistrust from practical factions.

### Machine Concord

Theme: automation, drones, efficient logistics.  
Benefits: production, drones, stockpile optimization.  
Costs: cold reputation, strange demands.

### Dust Clans

Theme: piracy, independence, survival.  
Benefits: fences, intimidation, ambush tools.  
Costs: lawful hostility.

## 4. Standing

Standing is player-to-Faction relationship.

Suggested player-facing bands:

- hated;
- distrusted;
- neutral;
- useful;
- trusted;
- sworn;
- champion.

Effects:

- job access;
- prices;
- services;
- patrol behavior;
- black-market access;
- shared-goal rewards;
- NPC dialog.

Standing should move through actions:

- completing faction jobs;
- contributing goods;
- helping allies;
- harming enemies;
- smuggling or piracy;
- story choices.

---

## 5. Influence

Influence is Faction power over a Place, Route, Region, or Market.

Influence affects:

- station services;
- route safety;
- customs pressure;
- market modifiers;
- job generation;
- shared-goal availability;
- local Events.

Influence is not the same as player Standing.

---

## 6. Shared Goals

Shared Goals are faction or station projects that multiple players/NPCs can advance.

Goal examples:

- Mercy Relay Repair Fund;
- stabilize Red Maw route;
- evacuate quarantine station;
- suppress pirate den;
- build deep-nebula beacon;
- open or seal Dead Gate.

A good shared goal shows:

- progress;
- needed contributions;
- who benefits;
- what world state changes;
- what reward participants get.

---

## 7. Contribution Types

Players can contribute:

- Commodities;
- credits;
- completed jobs;
- scan data;
- salvage items;
- route-clearing Bounties;
- outpost production;
- faction standing milestones.

NPCs can contribute in low-pop worlds so goals do not stall forever.

---

## 8. Faction Jobs

Faction jobs are ordinary Contracts/Bounties with faction context.

Examples:

- Mourning Union: deliver Med Gel, repair relay, evacuate refugees.
- Helix Cartel: move restricted goods, manipulate Market, bribe official.
- Black Office: scan smuggler route, clear pirate buoy, inspect cargo.
- Saints: recover relic, map anomaly, decode signal.
- Machine Concord: deliver drone brains, optimize stockpile, install beacon.
- Dust Clans: raid convoy, plant decoy, fence cargo.

---

## 9. Faction Conflict

Faction conflict should be visible and local before becoming global.

Early conflict:

- Union vs Cartel over Mercy Relay supply.
- Black Office vs Dust Clans over route safety.
- Saints vs Concord over relic interpretation.

Later campaign:

- regional fronts;
- route control;
- Dead Gate outcome.

---

## 10. UX Requirements

Faction screens must answer:

- Who are they?
- How do they feel about me?
- What do they want?
- What changes if I help?
- What will their enemies think?

Avoid abstract bars without consequences.

---

## 11. Low-Pop Scaling

Shared goals must scale:

- smaller requirements;
- NPC contributions;
- longer expiry;
- solo-friendly subgoals;
- meaningful partial rewards.

---

## 12. Recommended First Faction Slice

M7:

- activate Mourning Union and Helix Cartel first;
- add standing bands;
- add one Union shared goal: Mercy Relay Repair Fund;
- add one Cartel job that conflicts indirectly;
- completion affects one Market or Route;
- show Faction Overview screen.

Defer:

- all six factions fully active;
- regional fronts;
- permanent allegiance lock;
- faction PvP.
